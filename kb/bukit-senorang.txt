PESTS AND DISEASES. Rats: PA Table 13 baiting with Storm (flocoumafen 0.05%) in 2018 and "2019" (means 2020), 184.72 ha, 7 and 8 rounds; RM22/ha. Barn owl boxes (PA Table 14) 142 vs 149 required, 1 per 11 ha, 22 occupied (15%), versus over 90% at neighbouring Bukit Jin Estate; cause to be investigated. Agro: random census found over 20% fresh damage in P18 and P22; no census in P22, irregular in P18/P20; one baiting round in P22 on 15 April 2026 with no replacement; systematic block-by-block baiting with 7-day replacement until acceptance below 20%. Estate census figures of 80-100% damage in P01/P02 called questionable; use platform census with 20% threshold in tall palms. PA says no serious rat attack. Bagworm: 2025 outbreak in P18 controlled with Dipel (67.91 ha treated, 4 rounds); bagworm RM152/ha, 186% over the RM53 budget; total pests and diseases RM198/ha vs 151. No reinfestation (both reports). Ganoderma: serious in old palms; mounding done in P05; no updated census since the previous visit (Agro); annual GPS census advised, half-yearly where over 5%.

PRUNING. PA Table 16: progressive pruning paid every 3 months, all rounds 100%; fronds stacked between 4 palms; slight over-pruning in young palms (2018B). Cost RM165/ha vs 161. Agro: P01, P02, P05 acceptable, keep one subtending frond; P18 and P20 up to date but over-pruning in P20 and fronds left in circles in P20B; P22 under-pruned with long frond butts and fronds in circles, must be completed.

YOUNG MATURE (Agro section 5). P22: stunted, pale palms on silty, compacted, waterlogged soil; leaf N 2.40%, P 0.153%, K 1.34%, Mg 0.27%, B 17.93 ppm, an uptake problem; advice scupper drains, compost or EFB 40 t/ha, subsoil manuring. P20: small palms on gravelly hills, mild K and Mg deficiency. P18 at 170 SPH needs hexagonal thinning. P05 low-lying areas pale, mild N and K deficiency on laterite P05D.

IMMATURE. PR2025 (129.66 ha, ex 1998A/B/E and 2000A): planted by June 2025 at 148 SPH after weather delays and a July 2024 seedling phytotoxicity incident; watermelon intercrop (grower has now stopped per PA); circles need weeding; first ablation done. PR2026A-C (101.58 ha): Agro Table 15 (April 2026) 12,921 of 15,034 palms planted (86%), land preparation delayed by the contractor; Mucuna only 40%, so keep soft grasses. PA (June) says planting completed, watermelon resumed on 50 ha, census needed. PR2026D-G (179.60 ha): felled and deboled, infilling under way; plant October-November 2026.

DRAINS, ROADS (PA). Drains RM1/ha; desilting needed in low-lying young mature area. Road grading budget RM20,184, nothing done; roads RM89/ha vs 219.

VEHICLES (PA Table 24). 10 farm and 12 mini tractors; WXG 4457 and 3 mini tractors unused, to be sold. Six farm tractors over budget per hour (worst BQD 3982 RM37.66 vs 11.10), mainly repairs from under-use.

RAINFALL (Agro Appendix 2, May-April years). FY21/22 3,080.93 mm, 112 days; FY22/23 2,456.19 mm, 113; FY23/24 2,858.05 mm, 108; FY24/25 2,991.79 mm, 161; FY25/26 May-March only 1,354.92 mm, 125 days (April blank); July 2025 only 8.4 mm.
