The financial year runs May to April, so all "year" figures below are FY periods (FY2025/26 = May 2025 to April 2026). Single estate, single division (Division: Main); no combined figures.

AREA (PA Table 1 and 2, Agro Table 1, as of April 2026): title 844.68 ha; planted 761.16 ha (90.11% of title), all mature, no immature, no replanting; roads 73.90 ha, buildings/others 9.62 ha. Plantings: 2000 74.00 ha (A 33.54, B 40.46), 2001 50.12 (A), 2002 93.08 (A 54.28, B 38.80), 2003A 64.22 (1 20.89, 2 43.33), 2003B 89.56 (1 11.04, 2 32.65, 3 45.87), 2005 57.88 (A 29.12, B 28.76), 2010 117.17 (A 32.13, B 44.62, C 15.49, D 24.93), 2011 64.92 (A 42.10, B 22.82), 2012 141.19 (A 54.26, B 47.06, C 39.87), 2014 9.02 (A 5.46, B 3.56). All DxP (IOI; 2005 Guthrie, 2010 Sime Darby, 2014 mixed). PA: no replanting for the next two years since the 26-year-old 2000 planting is manageable; 2000A and 2000B yields to April 2026 were 25.65 and 23.06 t/ha. Agro: about 10% of palms over 25 years; replanting P00 not recommended yet (yield satisfactory, palms short); 20 to 25-year-olds are the largest share; prime plantings 44% of area. Agro: assess pond water supply against peak irrigation demand; bring in Ganoderma-free soil medium.

WEEDING (PA): Circle and path spraying 1,001.46 of 2,283.48 ha (field rows actually add to 1,002.62 ha), 1.3 of 3.0 rounds (44%): 2000 1.5 rounds, 2002 and 2003A 2.0, 2005 1.8, others 1.0. Delay because sprayers were sent to help harvesting in the high-crop first half; some fields deliberately deferred since circles were clean. Target Tetracera, Stenochlaena, VOPs and woody shrubs at terrace edges; consider CDA for circles. Agro Table 6: inter-row 1,542.45 of 2,283.48 ha (68%), circle 1,002.62 of 2,329.12 ha (43%); most fields only 2 interrow and 1 circle round; overall 55% of programme, due to labour diverted to harvesting and deferment. Second circle round not completed in most fields, especially prime plantings. Remove large woodies by slashing or triclopyr plus diesel.

PESTS AND DISEASES: Rats: insignificant (Agro Table 7 monthly damage 1 to 6%), no baiting; some damage near platforms in prime plantings. Barn owls (PA Table 13): 76 boxes, 1 per 10 ha; occupancy Feb 2026 23 boxes (30%), still low; no cleaning done, clean yearly. Agro: damaged boxes replaced, census again in six months. Bagworm: Promax (deltamethrin) 10 ml per 10 L on 38 ha in 2012A and 2012C, 2 rounds (PA); no recurrence (Agro). Predator host plants seen but not systematically recorded (PA). Ganoderma (census Oct 2025, PA Table 15 and Agro Table 8): 8,348 infected of 96,083 palms on 717.47 ha, about 9% (PA) / 8% (Agro). Worst: 2001A 18% (1,163 palms), 2000B 13%, 2000A 12%, 2003A1 19% (PA) or 12% (Agro), 2005A 10%; lowest 2010D and 2014B 3%. 2003B1 and B2 not in the census. PA: standing-palm basis understates the true rate (dead and fallen palms not counted). Agro: over 5% in most fields; new census started; mitigation by palm mounding in P10, P11, P12 and deboling; sanitation at replanting for older fields. Mounding: 3,750 palms in 2010A at RM7.20 = RM27,000; about 3,000 more to do (PA). Cattle intrusion unresolved, damage to lower fronds in P12A/B (Agro). Census and thinning: nil spent of RM7,684 budget; use it for censuses and thinning runts in 2010 to 2014. Agro: thinning in P11 improved light.
