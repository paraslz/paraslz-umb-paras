ARWANA ESTATE (INTI), PT Lifere Agro Kapuas (PT LAK), Kalimantan, UMB group. Fact pack from PA Report 2/2026 (Planting Advisor Mr. Boey Chee Weng, visit 18 and 19 August 2026, review period May 2025 to April 2026, Financial Year 2025/2026) and Agronomy Report 1/2026 (Agronomist Mr. Chong Kuan Ming, visit 20 May 2026, programme 2026/2027; previous agronomy visit 27 and 28 November 2025). Plantation Director Mr. Tee Tong Heng, Senior Manager Bpk Deni Irawan, Estate Manager Bpk Ismanto Arga. The financial year runs May to April. UMB Director Mr. Tee Lip Zhun, the CEO of UMB and Deputy COO Encik Nazaruddin of Far East Holding joined the PA visit on 19 August. 4-month drought at the PA visit.

AREA (PA section 2): title 4,483.38 ha. Mature 1,000.73 ha (22.32%), immature 449.61 ha (10.03%), total oil palm 1,450.34 ha (32.35%). Non-cultivated 3,033.04 ha: overhead cable/drain 46.77, ex PLG 29.25, building sites and roads 92.76, vacant plantable 1,733.04, vacant unplantable 1,131.22. No change in area. The last 35 ha of the 173 ha rehabilitation was finished in April 2026; 177 ha is to be rehabilitated in FY2026/27, 69 ha done by August, 108 ha by April 2027. Of the 1,000.73 ha declared mature only 589 ha was productive (PA 2.4). Agronomy Table 1 by planting year: 2013 353.85 ha, 2014 74.16, 2018 88.41, 2019 375.43, 2020 410.23 (Table 2: 410.32), 2023 47.63, 2024 85.49, 2025 15.05, total 1,450.34. 167 sub-blocks averaging 8.7 ha (2025 planting 12 sub-blocks of 1.3 ha). About 54% of the area is young mature (4 to 7 years), 36% prime mature, 10% immature; no old plantings, P13 the oldest, no replanting needed. Sub-block consolidation still not done (Agro).

Palms supplied: 2023 11,085, 2024 12,372, 2025 6,418, total 29,875 (2019 planting 11,667, 2020 13,456). PA 6.7.1: 8,435 palms supplied against 3,466 budgeted in FY2025/26. Supplying on hold until Seluang nursery seedlings are ready; full census needed (Agro 2.10).

Staff unchanged: EM, Asisten Kepala Rafael Raya (from 1 Jan 2026), two division assistants, KTU.

WEEDING (PA): circle spraying 2,596 ha (2.6 rounds), 86% of 3,002 ha (3 rounds) to April; circles generally clean so some rounds deferred. High-volume CKS, 3.1 ha/man-day, 18 pumps a day, about 6 hours worked (village workers); piece rate RM14.44/ha, earnings RM44.76/day vs basic RM33.45. Glyphosate 0.45 l/ha with Ally 0.02 kg/ha; advised to drop Ally and use VLV nozzles. Selective spraying 2,948 ha (2.9 rounds), 98% of 3,002 ha; inter-rows well sprayed; rehab areas to be sprayed every 2 months against Stenochlaena and sedges. CKS 3 ha/day, knapsack power sprayers 3.1 ha/day (should reach 4 to 5). Paraquat 0.45 l/ha + Ally 0.018 kg/ha by CKS; Paraquat 0.25 l/ha + Garlon 0.75 l/ha by knapsack, 15 pumps a day. Refilling water from field drains wastes time; use pre-mixed jerry cans. President Director: replace paraquat with Triclopyr by mist blower once Stenochlaena and VOPs are gone. Agronomy (Tables 9, 11): 2 rounds inter-row and circle done everywhere in FY2025/26, 3rd round incomplete in several P13 and young mature blocks; ground cover improved, VOPs largely gone; over-spraying (bare ground) still in AQ14 and AR14 (P18) and AH76, AH77, AH79 (P20); broadleaf weeds in AQ15; Clidemia hirta, jungle woodies, lallang and Stenochlaena still in patches; keep Nephrolepis; no path spraying; Garlon-diesel or slashing for woodies.

MANURING: PA 6.3.2: 167 of 550 rounds applied in FY2025/26; estate used up old stock without ordering new, and new fertiliser came late (transport problems from higher diesel prices), only delivered in FY2026/27. Broadcast at 20 bags/man-day, RM2.05/bag, earnings RM41/day. Recommends mini crawler or Badang on the flat terrain. New drains 3,531 m, 9,466 m and 42 m (RM42, 69, 74 per chain), unbudgeted. Six new water gates finished Sep 2025 at RM2,477 each. Agro: 9.5% of planted area is low-lying and flood-prone, floods subside within 7 days; keep water table 50 to 75 cm.

PRUNING: PA text 253,266 of 417,304 palms pruned to April (61%); PA table 189,834 of 508,314 (37%). Progressive pruning RM0.72/palm/year, corrective RM1.92/palm; quality satisfactory. Agro Table 8 (P13/P14, 2026 to May): 382.23 of 642.02 ha (60%), AQ13 only 30%, AQ12 89%; under-pruning in AQ13, AQ14; fronds stacked too close to circles (AQ15). Keep two subtending fronds; do not prune retarded palms until the lowest bunch is 3 feet up. Poison abnormal unproductive palms.
