SOUTH EAST PAHANG ESTATE, South East Pahang Oil Palm Berhad (United Malacca Berhad group, UMB). Fact pack from PA Report 1/2026 (Planting Advisor En Adnan Bin Mustafa, visit 25 June 2026, review period May 2025 to April 2026, 12 months, financial year FY2025/26; previous PA visit 9 December 2025) and Agronomy Report 1/2026 (Senior Agronomist Mr Lai Hong Heng, visit 7 May 2026, previous agronomy visit 31 December 2025; manuring programme FY2026/27). Plantation Controller (Peninsular Malaysia) Mr Low Fook Sin. Estate Senior Manager Ts. Mohamed Rezal Bin Bakal (PA cover spells Bakar), who also manages Bukit Senorang Estate. The estate financial year runs May to April. Single estate; not a combined report.

AREA (PA Table 1, as of April 2026; Agro Tables 1-3): Estate title 1,618.73 ha. Planted 1,522.07 ha, all mature (93% of title): 2005 planting 483.03 ha (126 SPH), 2006 planting 614.23 ha (137 SPH), 2007 planting 424.81 ha (146 SPH). Roads 89.64 ha, buildings/river 7.02 ha (buildings, ex-nursery, orchards, football fields). No change since last visit and no replanting planned in the next two financial years (PA). Agro: 72% of palms aged 20-25, 28% prime. Sub-blocks (Agro Table 2): P2005 A-M (13), P2006 A-P (16), P2007 A-K (11). Agro asks for a new palm census. Runt palms (P07K) not removed in FY2025/26; Agro advises 50 mL glyphosate trunk injection.

Manuring (10) and spraying (13) workers also work on Bukit Senorang Estate.

WEEDING (PA): Contracted out, 13 workers who also spray Bukit Senorang. Circle and path spraying (Table 7): programme 4,566.21 ha at 3 rounds; 2,853.50 ha done, 62%, 1.87 rounds; 2005 3.00 rounds (100%), 2006 1.50 rounds (921.35 ha, printed 33%), 2007 1.16 rounds (39%). Selective (Table 9): 3,898.00 of 4,566.21 ha, 85%, 2.56 rounds; 2005 and 2007 3.00 rounds, 2006 1.93 rounds (64%), intentionally delayed by field condition; broadleaves regenerating in 2006. Output 5.0 ha per man-day at RM15/ha, RM75/day; could return to 7.0 ha/day. Agro Table 8 shows interrow 3 rounds and circle 2 rounds all 100% in every field. Agro: vegetation about 50% soft grasses and 50% Nephrolepis; Nephrolepis dominant in P05M, P07I, P07H, P07K, P07J, P07E, P07F, P07D (beneficial, suited to silty wet soils, do not over-spray); soft grasses sparse with weeds in P06F, P06I, P06E, P06P, P06O, P05K, P06N, P05E, P05D, P05B. Vacant points overgrown (P07E, P06F, P05D, P05J). For Stenochlaena/Dicranopteris: 150 mL Garlon + 25 g Ally per 10 L water by mist blower with one-sided nozzle cover, rotated with 200 mL Plantek (32.1% triclopyr-butotyl), 3 rounds a year.

Productivity: semi-mechanised 30 bags/man-day (target 40), sub-surface 10 (target 20). Cost RM1,523.15/ha, 5% below budget. Agro Table 4 (at May 2026): programme 2,023.17 mt (Mix B 1,696.18, kieserite 13.55, RP 313.44), delivered 1,866.45, applied 1,702.75 mt (Mix B 1,546.77, kieserite 13.55, RP 142.43), printed 91%. Delayed in flood-prone P07A-G and P07K by flooding Nov 2025 to Feb 2026; completion expected end June 2026. EMDEK spreader on 1,390.55 ha (91.4%), sub-soil on 131.52 ha mainly P07.

MOUNDING, FRONDS AND SOIL (both): PA Table 16 palm base soil mounding FY2025/26: 2005 52,035 palms (479.92 ha, 100%), 2006 12,508 palms (118.97 ha, PA says 90%), total 64,543 palms at RM7.20 = RM464,709; RM450/ha, unbudgeted; remaining 2007 next year. Agro Table 13: P05 52,014 (85%), P06 54,843 (65%), P07 none, total 106,857 palms (52.8%). Agro strongly objects to slant-cut trenches toward the harvesting path (P06F, P06P, P05B, P05L): severe root damage, 4-5 ft trenches in the fertiliser zone may lose over 30% of EMDEK-spread fertiliser; discontinue immediately and dig straight trenches parallel to the harvesting path. Fronds placed in trenches (P05B, P05L) do not give the intended runoff barrier and organic fertiliser zone. Subsoil has high silt (P05C, P06M), poor drainage and aeration; palms pale green to yellowish. No de-silting in FY2024/25 or FY2025/26; annual targeted de-silting advised.
