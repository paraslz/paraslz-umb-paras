SELUANG ESTATE (INTI), PT Lifere Agro Kapuas (PT LAK), Kalimantan, UMB group. The estate's financial year runs May to April, so "FY 2025/26" means May 2025 to April 2026. Money as printed (RM).

AREA (PA 2/2026, area statement as at April 2026; no change since last visit): mature 1,116.66 ha (46.99%), immature 129.61 ha (5.45%), total oil palm 1,246.27 ha (52.44%). Young mature palms 51.9% and prime 43.8% of area. 305.19 ha is low-lying and flood-prone (Agro 3.7 says 25.34%, 5.4.10 says 24.52%).

If confirmed at 97-128 SPH, Agro recommends a feasibility study on further infilling now, before canopy closure makes supply ineffective.

STAFF (PA): manager, KTU, four division assistants and an Askep (Rafael Raya, from 2 Jan 2026); adequate, no change.

WEEDING (PA): circle spraying 2,518.59 ha (2.3 rounds), 68% of 3,698 ha budgeted (3 rounds); deferred where circles were clean; circles generally clean. CKS high-volume output only 2.5 ha/man-day (potential 5), sprayers refill from field drains; piece rate RM14.44/ha, earnings RM36.10/day vs basic RM33.54; glyphosate 0.45 L/ha plus Ally 0.02 kg/ha (PA advises dropping Ally); 18 pumps a day, about 6 hours worked. Advice: VLV low-volume nozzles, jerry cans of pre-mixed water from the chemical store, one circle round converted to raking where needed. Selective spraying 2,285.37 ha (2 rounds), 62% of 3,698 ha (the printed total leaves out Division 1B); CKS and knapsack power sprayer both 3 ha/man-day, earnings RM43.22; paraquat 0.45 L/ha plus Ally 0.02 (CKS) or 0.01 kg/ha (power sprayer). Interrows well under control.
Agronomy 1/2026: two rounds of circle and selective spraying completed in FY 2025/26, third round only in part. Woody weeds much reduced, but sparse or bare ground from over-spraying/blanket spraying is now dominant (SR23-13C1, SR23-13A1, SR22-13A1, SO23-19A1, SO14-23B1, SP14-13B1) and soft grass cover has fallen. Bracken ferns (Stenochlaena palustris, Dicranopteris linearis) in low-lying areas; encourage Nephrolepis (as at UMB SEP Estate). Mist blowers: one-sided covers in sparse areas, round covers in tall grass.

MANURING: PA says 226 of 261 rounds budgeted were applied, delayed by late Mix B delivery; broadcast, 20 bags/man-day, RM2.05/bag, earnings RM41/day; recommends rubber-track Badang semi-mechanical application. Compost 85.03 of 590.2 ha budgeted (labour shortage), 20 t/ha, 50 palms/man-day at RM1.08/palm (earnings RM54). LEAF AND SOIL (Agro): most fields deficient in N and P, localised K shortfall, Mg sufficient to high; key constraints low P and marginal N, addressed in the FY2026/27 programme. Leaf samples Oct 2025: N 1.97-2.77%, P 0.124-0.176%. Palm vigour much improved since late 2025.

PEST AND DISEASE: PA says rat census below the 10% threshold so no baiting, but its table shows SP13-13A1 fresh damage 21.40% and SM21-13A2 14.81%. Agro Table 10 (Nov-Dec 2025, Mar 2026): estate total old damage 3.53%, fresh 5.07% on 31,678 palms sampled; Div 1A 1.88%/2.92%, Div 2 4.45%/6.27%; SO14-13B1 old 11.43%. Estate baited in January 2026 where fresh damage exceeded its 5% action level, replacing bait every 3-4 days; Agro wants a second-generation bait lasting 7 days. Fresh damage now minimal. No other pests or diseases seen (PA).
