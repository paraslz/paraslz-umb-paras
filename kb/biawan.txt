CIRCLE SPRAYING (PA 6.2.1): 5,038 ha (1.9 rounds) or 62% of 8,168 ha (3 rounds) to April; many circles were clean and deferred. Circles generally clean. CKS high-volume, 4.8 ha/man-day; sprayers work only 5 hours and refill from field drains. Advice: 8-hour day, jerry cans of pre-mixed water at the chemical store, VLV low-volume nozzles, glyphosate only without Ally, time and motion study on pumps per man-day. Piece rate RM13.23/ha, earnings RM63.50/day vs basic RM33.34. Chemicals paraquat 0.33 l/ha and Ally 0.01 kg/ha (2.5 pumps/ha).
SELECTIVE SPRAYING (PA 6.2.2): 8,104 ha (2.9 rounds) or 99% of 8,168 ha. Inter-rows satisfactory except spots of jungle woodies; Stenochlaena and sedges well sprayed so inter-rows lack soft grass; soft grasses or Nephrolepis must now be allowed to dominate. CKS 4.8 ha/man-day; knapsack power sprayer 4.9 (expected 5 to 6). Piece rate RM13.23/ha; earnings RM63.50 (CKS) and RM64.82 (power sprayer) vs basic RM33.45. CKS paraquat 0.39 l/ha plus Ally 0.01 kg/ha only for noxious ferns and sedges; power sprayer Garlon 0.2 l/ha. 13 pumps per man-day, equal to 5 hours work.
WEEDING (Agro 4.4, Table 9, FY2025/26 on 2,725.95 ha): interrow rounds 99%, 75%, 28%; circle rounds 98%, 95%, 55%. Ground vegetation improved vs 2-2025 but mostly bare ground with sparse soft grasses; bare area has increased (BM43-16B1, BM44-12A1, BN48-13A1, BQ37-13A1, BR38-12A1, BR43-13A1), raising erosion and runoff risk. Patches of Stenochlaena palustris and Dicranopteris linearis. Flood-prone low-lying area 218.5 ha (7.4% of 2,932.9 ha; Table 10, 13% of 1,578.50 ha surveyed) favours bracken ferns; Nephrolepis better adapted but sparse; promote it, avoid blanket spraying. Over-spraying in BM44-12A1 and BO51-14A1; incomplete kill in BN33-15B1. BP31-13A1 much improved. Use mist blowers with one-sided shield (sparse) or round shield (dense weeds); keep a 3 to 4 ft circle.
